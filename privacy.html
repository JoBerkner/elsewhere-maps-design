What is stored and why

When you send the form, your Etsy order number and your choices for the map (centre, title, language, units, cities and special places) are stored so that I can design and print your map (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, performance of a contract). Your IP address is used only for the security check and to block abuse and is not stored with your design. Your data is used only for your Etsy order: no newsletter, no advertising, no sharing with anyone except the print partner, who receives only the finished print file.

Services used

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) hosts this page and the website elsewhere-maps.com and receives the usual connection data when you open them. Neither site uses cookies, tracking or analytics.
  • Scaleway (Scaleway SAS, France, data centre in Paris) receives the form, checks it and passes it on. The security check against automated abuse (ALTCHA) runs in your browser and on this server, without cookies and without any third party.
  • GitHub stores your design as an entry in a private repository that only I can access.
  • OpenStreetMap (OpenStreetMap Foundation, UK) delivers the map tiles and the place search. Your browser connects to its servers when you use the maps.
